# Translation attribution

Google Translate badge PNGs are unmodified official 2x assets from
https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/translate/images/google-translate-attribution.zip
downloaded 2026-10-27. Google marks remain the property of Google LLC and are used
according to https://docs.cloud.google.com/translate/attribution and
https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/language/translation/translation-terms.

The app uses Google Translate through ML Kit for on-device Android translation.
Unmodified Google-derived results retain an attribution marker across review,
packages, LAN snapshots and offline quiz records. Text is translated locally;
model downloads are explicit. Users must review machine output before publishing.
Google does not warrant the accuracy, reliability, suitability or noninfringement
of translation results. Windows CTranslate2/Argos results are marked separately.

Model/runtime versions, origins, hashes and license notices are documented in
docs/translation-runtime.md. Downloaded runtime/model archives retain their
upstream copyright and license files; model binaries are not bundled here.
